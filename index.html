01 — THE ARTIST

A hand that refuses to settle on a single material.

Watercolour and terracotta, glazed ceramics and hand-painted cloth, sand drawn in light, the digital tablet, and installations large enough to walk through — Kavita Jha’s work moves freely across all of them, held together by a recurring cast of faces, gods, fish and many watching eyes.

What follows is one long corridor through that body of work, medium by medium. Take it slowly.
