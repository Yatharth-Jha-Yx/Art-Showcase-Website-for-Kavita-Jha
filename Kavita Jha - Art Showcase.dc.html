About the artist

Kavita Jha is a Mumbai-born artist who refuses to settle on a single material.

Watercolour and terracotta, glazed ceramics and hand-painted cloth, sand drawn in light, the digital tablet, and installations large enough to walk through — the work moves freely across all of them, held together by a recurring cast of faces, gods, fish and many watching eyes.

What follows is a journey through that body of work, grouped by medium. Take it slowly, top to bottom.
